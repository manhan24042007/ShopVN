Return Policy

Flexible return policy for our customers

7 Days

Return Period

Free

Return Shipping

Refund

Within 3-5 days

Return Conditions

  • Product must be in original condition, unused, with all tags and warranty card attached
  • Product has manufacturer defects (broken, defective zipper, color issues...)
  • Product delivered differs from order (wrong size, color, or model)
  • Return request must be submitted within 7 days of receiving the order

Non-Returnable Items

  • Products that have been used, dry cleaned, altered or customized
  • Products damaged by improper use or storage by the customer
  • Discounted items, Flash Sale items, clearance sale
  • Accessories, underwear, personal care items

Return Process

Step 1: Submit Request

Contact us via hotline 1900 1234 or email support@globalmart.com with photos of the defective product.

Step 2: Confirmation

Our staff will confirm and guide you on how to send the item to our warehouse.

Step 3: Inspection

Warehouse will inspect the product within 1-2 business days.

Step 4: Completion

Refund will be processed to your bank account or exchanged for another product.
